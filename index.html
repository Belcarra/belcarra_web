Why Hardware OEMs Choose Belcarra

Trusted by leading device manufacturers to eliminate driver development risk and deliver frictionless customer connectivity.

Zero-Privilege End-User Access

Traditional serial (COM) ports require custom software and elevated permissions. Belcarra provides a standard Ethernet interface over USB, allowing your customers to configure devices via any web browser (http://192.168.x.x) without needing IT administrator privileges.

Full Windows 11 AMD64 & ARM64 Native

Tested, optimized, and signed for modern Windows 11 on both ARM64 (Qualcomm Snapdragon X Elite, Microsoft Surface Pro) and AMD64 (x64), alongside long-term support for Windows 10.

Branded Turnkey OEM Installation Kits

Delivered as custom redistributable installation packages branded with your company name and USB-IF Vendor ID (VID) and Product ID (PID) for seamless, silent end-user deployment.

Multi-Protocol & Composite Flexibility

Auto-detects and supports CDC-NCM (high-throughput), CDC-EEM (microcontrollers), CDC-ECM, and RNDIS. Co-exists seamlessly with serial (CDC-ACM), audio, or mass storage sub-functions.

The Challenge of In-House Drivers

  • Maintenance Burden: Continuous OS update testing, breaking kernel changes, and porting to emerging ARM64 chipsets.
