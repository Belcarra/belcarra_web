Evaluation Driver Checklist

All evaluation requests are reviewed by our senior driver engineering team. Delivery typically occurs within 1–2 business days.

3. USB Vendor ID (VID) & Product ID (PID)

Commercial USB devices require unique 16-bit Vendor IDs from USB.org. For prototype evaluation, Belcarra can bind drivers to your assigned VID/PID or provide test IDs.

4-digit hexadecimal assigned by USB.org

4. DHCP Service & Addressing Configuration

USBLAN includes an optional built-in DHCP service to automatically assign IPv4 addresses to the host and device across the point-to-point USB connection.

Default is 169.254.1.1. Specify an alternate subnetwork if desired (e.g., 192.168.225.1).
