Belcarra published transfer-rate comparisons between Microsoft's RNDIS approach and Belcarra USBLAN.

The objective was to provide measured performance context for protocol and architecture decisions. Raw throughput is only one part of integration quality, but it remains important for products with strict data movement requirements.

These results help engineering teams reason about tradeoffs in protocol choice, framing behavior, and implementation details that can influence effective bandwidth over USB links.

When applying comparison data, teams should validate on their own hardware, firmware, and workload profile before locking product assumptions.
