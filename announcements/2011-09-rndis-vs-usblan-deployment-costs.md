Belcarra discussed deployment-cost implications when comparing RNDIS and USBLAN approaches.

Beyond protocol mechanics, deployment complexity can drive significant engineering and operational cost over a product lifetime. Packaging, update flow, support burden, and field recovery procedures all contribute to total cost of ownership.

This post framed protocol selection as both a technical and program-management decision. Teams should account for long-term servicing overhead, not only initial bring-up effort.

Cost-sensitive evaluation criteria:
- Integration and validation effort.
- Support case complexity and mean time to resolution.
- Update and rollback safety for field deployments.
