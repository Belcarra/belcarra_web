Contact Us

For OEM integration, technical support, and licensing inquiries, contact Belcarra.

Email: info@belcarra.com

Best use of first contact

  • State whether the request is technical support, integration guidance, or commercial licensing.
  • Include urgency, deployment stage, and target timeline.
  • Provide a primary technical contact for follow-up.

Technical request checklist

  • Windows version/build and architecture (amd64 or arm64)
  • USBLAN driver package version
  • Device firmware version and selected protocol mode
  • Observed behavior and expected behavior
  • Reproduction steps and relevant logs

Commercial request checklist

  • Company and product context
  • Planned deployment volume and timeline
  • Required protocol support (RNDIS, ECM, EEM, NCM)
  • Any certification or compliance constraints
